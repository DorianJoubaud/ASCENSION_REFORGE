\section*{Broader Impact Statement}

We evaluated ASCENSION on the publicly available UCR Time
Series Classification archive (102 datasets spanning sensor,
motion, image-derived, biomedical, and spectral domains) and
identified no significant ethical concerns in its use.
% REVISED FOR TMLR (rebuttal C3)
By providing a low-risk augmentation default, with a positive mean
gain on each of the three classifiers under a fixed configuration
and its benefit concentrated on the smallest datasets, ASCENSION
lowers the cost of deploying TS classifiers when labelled data is
scarce, without requiring per-dataset method selection. The
generated samples remain
class-consistent by construction (via the rejection step), which
is especially valuable in scientific and operational settings
where labels are sensitive or costly to obtain.
